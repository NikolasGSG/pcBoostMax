Privacy policy

Effective 2026-04-28 · This page mirrors PRIVACY.md in the source repository.

The full plain-English policy lives alongside the source code. The headline summary is below; for the complete text, including data tables and your rights under GDPR / CCPA / UK GDPR / LGPD, please read the canonical document on GitHub.

Summary

  • GameBoost runs entirely on your computer.
  • It does not create accounts, does not require a login, and uploads nothing about your hardware, files, or settings.
  • The desktop app contains no third-party ad SDK and runs no JavaScript.
  • The in-app banner pulls a JSON manifest from this site and renders it as a native widget.
  • Clicking the banner opens your default browser at this site, where Google AdSense and Microsoft Advertising serve real ads — that's where their privacy policies apply.
  • You can switch personalized advertising off in Settings → Privacy; non-personalized inventory is served instead.
